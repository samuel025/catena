[[S1]]
Say you want a small loan in crypto. Today you usually have to lock up more money than you borrow. And even if you've paid back every loan on time on one network, the next network knows nothing about you. Catena fixes that. It gives you a credit history that follows you across networks, a bit like a credit score, and it's built on Reactive Network, which lets one smart program watch a blockchain and act on another within seconds.

[[S2]]
Meet a brand new user, with no history. They ask for twenty from a normal pool that wants collateral, and it says no. Our pool says no as well, but only because they're new. A new user is only allowed a small starter amount.

[[S3]]
Now they borrow a little and pay it back on time, on the first network. Watch the second network, which has never seen this person. Within a couple of seconds, Catena notices, scores them, and raises how much they can borrow, on both networks. First to ten, then to twenty-five.

[[S4]]
This is the dashboard, reading straight from the blockchains. Their trust level went up, and the borrowing limit is the same on both networks. It even shows what the score is based on, like on-time payments, and how long they've been around.

[[S5]]
Now the same loan of twenty that was refused before goes through, with no collateral at all, because of their track record.

[[S6]]
And it isn't all or nothing. If someone also puts up a little collateral, they can borrow more on top. Same collateral in both pools, but with a good history, they can borrow far more.

[[S7]]
What if someone doesn't pay back? Their limit drops to zero on both networks. That's a fixed rule, so the model can't talk its way around it. The network where it happened reacts at once, and the other one follows within a couple of seconds. We don't claim it's instant.

[[S8]]
Could someone borrow the maximum on both networks at the same moment? We tested exactly that. They hit their limit, and every extra attempt is refused.

[[S9]]
A few honest notes. It all runs on test networks, with fake money. The model learned from made-up data, and it's only about as accurate as a simple formula. So we don't claim it's smarter. We claim it works, it's transparent, and it runs entirely on-chain.

[[S10]]
Everything is open source. This is Catena, a credit history that follows you across blockchains.
