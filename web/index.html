Catena credit passport

One repayment record. Credit on every chain.

A small neural network inside a Reactive contract scores a wallet from its loan events on Base Sepolia and Unichain Sepolia, then sets its borrow quota on both chains. Test networks, fake money.

Loan eventA pool on Base or Unichain emits Borrowed, Repaid or Defaulted.
Reactive contractLasna updates the passport and runs the MLP in integer math.
Tier and quotasHard rules apply: defaults zero, one tier up per period.
CallbacksEach pool gets its new quota, usually within 1 to 3 seconds.
Catena is a hackathon prototype on test networks. Scores come from a model trained on synthetic data and are not real credit assessments. Explanations are model sensitivities, not definitive reasons.
